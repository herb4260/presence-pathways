Conceptual architecture

One event, multiple pathways

The design follows change from a situated practice and felt event into distinct processes. These pathways are hypotheses to investigate, not conclusions built into the instrument.

Research workflow

Ethnography first. Structure in service of depth.

Each layer answers a different question and creates an opportunity to revise the others.

Metaphysical humility

Study lived reality without claiming to prove or disprove divine action.

Non-pathologizing safety

Attend to agency, distress, control, sleep, functioning, and risk—not unusualness alone.

Public-data restraint

Keep identifiable testimony, locations, exact times, audio, and linkage keys outside GitHub.

Researcher reflexivity

Document how researchers' assumptions, social position, prior training, and expectations shape interpretation.
